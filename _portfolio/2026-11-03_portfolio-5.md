The workshop focused on practical approaches to organizing and managing performance indicator data using Google Sheets, along with leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) to streamline data analysis, visualization, and dashboard development. The goal was to help healthcare professionals improve the efficiency of performance monitoring, reporting, and data-driven decision-making.

It was a pleasure to share my knowledge and hands-on experience with the department's staff and explore how AI-powered tools can simplify everyday workflows. I sincerely appreciate the Department of Psychiatry for the invitation and the opportunity to exchange ideas on bringing data analytics and AI into healthcare operations.
