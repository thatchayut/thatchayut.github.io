The workshop was part of the eDENT-ELDER research initiative, which aims to develop an AI-powered platform for screening high-risk oral lesions among older adults in Thailand. The project brings together the International Dental Public Health Center, the Faculty of Engineering and Faculty of Dentistry at Chiang Mai University, and local healthcare partners to advance AI-driven oral health screening in community settings.

During the workshop, I had the pleasure of sharing knowledge and practical insights on the use of the eDENT-ELDER platform with dental professionals and village health volunteers. The training aimed to equip healthcare personnel with the skills needed to integrate AI-assisted oral health screening into their everyday practice.

It was a wonderful opportunity to contribute to the translation of AI research into real-world healthcare applications. I sincerely appreciate Associate Professor Dr. Patiwet Wuttisarnwattana, the project's principal investigator, and the Faculty of Engineering at Chiang Mai University for the invitation and the opportunity to be part of this collaborative initiative.
